1. Introduction

RH Ware ("we", "our", or "us") develops and maintains the Grid War mobile application (the "App", package rhware.gridwar.app). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal and device information when you download, install, or use Grid War.

By using Grid War, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Collection and Use

A. Personal Identifiable Information (None Collected)

We do not collect or solicit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your legal name, physical address, email address, phone number, contacts list, camera, microphone, or precise GPS location.

B. Local Gameplay Data (Stored On Your Device)

Your gameplay data—including match records, box captures, AI difficulty settings, win streaks, achievements, sound and vibration preferences, and virtual coin balances—is stored locally on your device via standard Android local storage (Room database and SharedPreferences). This data remains exclusively on your device and is not synchronized to external servers.

C. Device Diagnostics & Technical Telemetry

When you play the game, integrated third-party libraries may automatically log non-personally identifiable diagnostic data, such as device model, OS version, display metrics, network connectivity status, and crash logs to maintain game stability.

4. User Privacy Controls & Advertising Opt-Out

You have full autonomy over your data and advertising experience:

  • In-App Privacy Toggles: Navigate to Settings > Privacy & Ads inside Grid War to toggle Personalized Ads and Anonymous Analytics on or off.
  • Reset Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising Identifier anytime in your phone's system settings under Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Ad-Free Premium: You can completely eliminate all banner and interstitial advertisements by purchasing the Lifetime Ad-Free option.

5. In-App Purchases & Virtual Currency

Grid War offers virtual coins that can be earned through gameplay or purchased via Google Play Billing. Virtual coins have no real-world monetary value and cannot be redeemed, traded, or refunded outside of Google Play's standard refund procedures.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Grid War is an all-ages puzzle strategy game. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please notify us at support@rhware.app so we can take immediate corrective measures.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

All local gameplay data is retained on your device for as long as you keep the app installed. You may wipe all stored records at any time by clearing the application storage in Android system settings or by uninstalling the application.

8. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your location, you may have rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what information is processed, request deletion, or opt out of targeted advertising. We do not sell your personal data. You can exercise your choices directly within the in-app settings.
